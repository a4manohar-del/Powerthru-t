Auto ramp: 70 s of steady effort, then rising effort. Fatigue shows up as higher RMS and a falling median frequency. In Manual mode, keep the level low for the first 60 s so the baseline is learned at rest. Gaps appear when a simulated packet is lost; the app skips that second instead of guessing.
